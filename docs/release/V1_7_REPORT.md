## Numbers & Time

The original 39 records remain available to historical snapshots and explicit compatibility tests. Live generation uses `practice-numbers.js`: integer arithmetic, authored templates, stable value/direction namespaces, no external services. Cardinal formatting supports 0–9,999 (including masculine/feminine apocope/agreement). Default bands: A1.1 0–20; A1.2 0–100; A2 0–999; B 0–9,999. Larger ranges do not imply teaching financial registers or new grammatical systems.

Prices/clock practice require the A1.1 prices/time source or assumed earlier placement foundations. Dates require the A2.1 travel-comparison source or later assumed foundations. Prices are bounded by stage (20/99/499/999 euros plus cents); exact integer cents avoid floating-point grading. Times use all twelve hours at five-minute increments in both directions and audio, with accessible SVG labels and correct hour/minute hand angles. The formatter accepts hours 0–23; the displayed clock tasks explicitly use twelve-hour notation, without an ambiguous AM/PM test. Dates use all 365 valid non-leap day/month combinations, no impossible dates or floating year assumptions. Each sampled base value has visual-to-Spanish, Spanish-to-value and audio-to-value directions. All options are distinct and computed from valid nearby values. The pool is a bounded, deterministic representative sample, not every possible formatter value.

## Selection, evidence and compatibility

Ten unique exact models per round where available; unseen models outrank current-stage quota. A bounded 300-ID history accepts older 100-ID saves. Builder suppresses duplicate target strings across source slots; numeric directions share an exclusion identity. On exhaustion, oldest eligible models return. Broad concepts are penalised and narrow concepts normally capped at three when alternatives remain. Numerical mixed rounds cover available categories before allocating extra weakness-weighted slots, with one audio item. Recent course answers modestly increase relevant reconstruction priority. Current-stage slots remain a preference, never an excuse to cycle a depleted pool.

Mistakes record existing course/grammar evidence plus construction/correction or granular numeric categories and explicit grammatical persons where safely known. Hints remain assisted; recognition never claims independent recall. Same-day duplicate evidence is capped by the existing mastery model. After a miss, a different eligible related model may replace a slot two questions later; it does not change the checked item or round length. Weakness recommendations use actual evidence, not pool size. Results use familiar concept/category labels and do not imply permanent mastery.

Schema/key remain v2 / friends-to-amigos-v1. Optional validated practiceCategory/practicePerson snapshot fields are additive. Old snapshots are never regenerated or reinterpreted. Archived v1.6 Builder/Numbers attempts are import-tested. All old course/game/table/media/dictionary identities and required-path semantics remain unchanged. Internal game routes, Review, Saved Words, accent tolerance and distinct ñ are preserved.

## Limitations / human review

A small newly unlocked pool necessarily repeats; 185 total Spot scenarios cannot provide 200 never-repeated errors, and A1.1 alone has 30. Related contexts preserve concept repetition rather than promise no repetition forever. No new custom filter/setup surface is added. Builder remains finite reconstruction, not arbitrary word-order grading. Dates omit leap day, clocks explicitly use 12-hour semantics, and numeric sampling is bounded. Independent Spanish educator/native-speaker review of the new repairs, excerpt suitability and accepted order remains required. Physical keyboard/system bars and acoustic TTS need actual-device testing. No physical-device, emulator, human-audio or educator validation is inferred from automated tests.
