# External recommendation review — 26 October 2026

All 30 former catalogue records (29 distinct URLs) were checked against current public first-party information. Every retained/replacement item was reviewed; suitability remains editorial, not provider-certified CEFR. The app still shows 15 entries per level, three per category, from a bundled offline catalogue. Reuse supports short assisted samples early and retelling/independent comprehension later.

## Removed/replaced and normalised

- **Simon / RTVE → Cleo / RTVE series catalogue.** The former known November 2026 expiry is too close. Cleo's current provider availability states 31 December 2030. Availability is not guaranteed forever or outside supported regions.
- **Peppa Pig / RTVE → MUZZY BBC Spanish collection.** Replaces another known November 2026 expiry with a stable official collection. MUZZY is paid/account-based; a Spain-specific voice is not promised.
- **Linguriosa → Spanish Language Coach official site.** Current first-party details for the former entry could not be verified sufficiently in this environment. This is a verification decision, not a claim that the channel disappeared. The replacement offers identifiable podcasts, transcripts and YouTube vlogs; individual free/paid offers vary.
- **Dreaming Spanish** now links to its current official `dreaming.com/spanish` destination (two deliberately reused records). **Easy Spanish** and **Español con Juan** link to stable official creator sites instead of fragile channel handles.

Pocoyó's current RTVE listing states 30 April 2027; it is retained with explicit expiry metadata and must be reviewed again. Advanced RTVE programme/catalogue pages did not display a current expiry in the material retrieved; this is not a perpetual-rights claim. No expiring episode URL is used.

## Verification limits and maintenance

All six Steam store language tables were checked: Dorfromantik and Stardew Valley list Spain-Spanish interface/text; Unpacking and A Short Hike list Latin-American Spanish text; Firewatch and Return to Monkey Island list Spain-Spanish text/subtitles rather than Spanish voice. This release does not claim voiced Spanish for these games.

The four Difusión titles were individually corroborated through current official publisher search/index records (Vacaciones al sol, Sin noticias de Gurb, Un día en Madrid, Una nota falsa). Direct crawler access returned a bot restriction; live stock, checkout and delivery were not verified. The Spanish After Hours creator/channel identity was corroborated using official creator material, but a full channel inventory could not be retrieved. These are explicitly partial checks, not invented successful HTTP/playback tests. Other catalogue links resolved to their current official resource pages. Free public catalogues may still require account, age or regional checks; paid resources should be sampled before buying.

`npm run audit:recommendations` validates HTTPS/provider allowlist, identities, stage ranges, access/variety/review metadata, intentional duplicate rationale, stale dates (>90 days) and known expiry within 60 days. Optional `node scripts/audit-recommendations.cjs --network` is developer-only diagnostics; bot blocks/timeouts need human investigation and do not prove a resource is dead. Startup never fetches external resources. Recheck at least quarterly and before release; replace known near-expiry material even if it still loads today.
